Our story

Built for
this corner
of the desert

The Horizon Drip was born from a simple idea: Horizon City deserves a coffee shop as good as anywhere in El Paso — or anywhere at all. We source single-origin beans, pull clean espresso, and make every drink by hand.

Our drinks pull from the borderland — piloncillo, cinnamon, horchata, chile — flavors that belong here. Prices that make sense here too.
